# 🚲 Bike Sharing Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes Capital Bikeshare data to uncover rider behavior, revenue trends, seasonal patterns, weather impact, and operational insights. The project combines SQL, Python, and Power BI to build an end-to-end Business Intelligence solution that supports data-driven decision-making and demand forecasting.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

Bike-sharing operators face challenges in understanding rider demand, optimizing resources, and improving profitability. This project aims to:

* Analyze rider demand patterns
* Evaluate revenue and profit performance
* Understand weather and seasonal impacts
* Segment customers based on usage behavior
* Forecast bike demand using Machine Learning
* Provide actionable business insights through interactive dashboards

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

### Summer Drives Maximum Revenue

Summer consistently generated the highest revenue due to favorable weather and increased ridership.

### Strong Customer Loyalty

Registered riders contributed more than 80% of total ridership, indicating a stable customer base.

### Weather Significantly Impacts Demand

Clear weather conditions resulted in substantially higher ridership compared to extreme weather days.

### Rapid Year-over-Year Growth

The platform demonstrated strong growth in ridership and revenue across the analyzed period.

### Seasonal Profitability Differences

Profitability varied significantly across seasons, highlighting opportunities for seasonal pricing strategies.
